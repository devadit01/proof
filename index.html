Formulation, Optimization, and Comparative Evaluation of a Polyherbal Hydrogel Base Containing Salicylic Acid (2%) and Curcuma longa (Turmeric) Extract for Topical Applications

Abstract

This research details the formulation design, process optimization, and comprehensive physicochemical characterization of a topical polyherbal hydrogel system containing a synergistic combination of 2.0% w/w Salicylic Acid (keratolytic active) and 10.0% w/w Turmeric Extract (Curcuma longa, anti-inflammatory & antioxidant active). Utilizing Carbopol 940 (0.40% w/w) as a cross-linked gelling matrix, three experimental batches (F1, F2, F3) were formulated by varying co-solvent ratios and neutralizing agent concentrations to overcome the common dermatological limitations of conventional salicylic acid monotherapies.

The optimized formulation (F3) demonstrated superior physical stability, desirable pseudoplastic rheological flow (21,400 ± 350 cP), optimal spreadability (18.75 g·cm/s), and a skin-compatible pH value of 5.80 ± 0.15. Comparative evaluation against a marketed commercial 2% salicylic acid gel control revealed superior humectant moisture retention, complete absence of crystalline precipitation under polarized microscopy, and a non-greasy, soothing post-application skin feel. The findings establish that the developed polyherbal hydrogel delivers balanced keratolytic pore-clearing efficacy while mitigating erythema and desquamation through natural curcuminoids.

Extraction & Compounding

Compounding Chemistry & Dual-Action Mechanism

Four-phase pharmaceutical compounding protocol (Hydration, Active Solubilization, Botanical Integration, Polymer Neutralization) and dual keratolytic/anti-inflammatory pathways.

Standardized 4-Phase Compounding Protocol

1
Phase A: Polymer Hydration

Carbopol 940 (0.40% w/w) is slowly sprinkled uniformly over warm Purified Water and permitted to hydrate and swell for 12 hours to establish a clump-free polymer dispersion.

2
Phase B: Active Solubilization

Crystalline Salicylic Acid (2.0% w/w) is completely dissolved in a co-solvent mixture of Propylene Glycol (15.0%) and Methyl Propylene Glycol (1.0%) under magnetic agitation until optically clear.

3
Phase C: Botanical Incorporation

Standardized Turmeric Extract (10.0% w/w, Curcuma longa) is dispersed alongside Glycerine (3.0%) and Sorbitol (1.0%) into Phase B, yielding a vibrant, homogeneous curcuminoid solution.

4
Phase D: Controlled Blending

Phase C is introduced into the swollen Phase A polymer dispersion under continuous low-shear mechanical stirring to homogenize without inducing unwanted air entrapment or vortex foaming.

5
Phase E: TEA Neutralization

Triethanolamine (TEA) is titrated dropwise to neutralize carboxyl residues along the Carbopol backbone, expanding polymer chains into a coherent 3D viscoelastic hydrogel at pH 5.80.

6
Phase F: QC & Photoprotection

Rigorous characterization (centrifugation, pH electrode, Brookfield rheometer) followed by packaging into amber airless containers to shield bioactive curcuminoids against photodegradation.

Synergistic Dual-Active Mechanism on Inflammatory Acne-Prone Skin

Salicylic Acid (2.0% w/w)
  • Lipophilic Beta-Hydroxy Acid (BHA)
  • Penetrates deep into pilosebaceous ducts
  • Dissolves intercellular desmosomes
  • Rapid comedolytic & keratolytic clearance
  • Co-solubilized in PG matrix to prevent crystallization
+
Curcuma longa / Turmeric (10.0% w/w)
  • Bioactive curcuminoids (curcumin, demethoxycurcumin)
  • Suppresses NF-κB inflammatory transcription
  • Potent antimicrobial inhibition vs C. acnes
  • Free-radical scavenging antioxidant barrier
  • Humectant synergy mitigates dryness & erythema
